## PHILOSOPHY

Projects are the primary unit of work. Emails, meetings, GitHub activity, documents, calendar events, AI agents, and conversations all exist in service of a project. Dina should always attempt to associate new information with an existing project before creating a new one. Projects are living entities. They have purpose, momentum, blockers, history, people, and desired outcomes. The goal is not to manage tasks. The goal is to understand progress.

---

## FUTURE PROJECTS

When a new project is created, store: Mission; Desired outcome; Why it matters; Current phase; Priority; Key people; Important decisions; Current blockers; Waiting on Derek; Waiting on others; GitHub repositories; Related documents; Related conversations; Active AI agents; Last meaningful progress; Definition of success.

This document holds mission, phase, and philosophy — not the live numbered backlog. Open work items (“remaining tasks for Dina”, “mark 6 complete”) live in the **ProjectTask ledger** (chat tools: `list_project_tasks`, `add_project_task`, `complete_project_task`). Waiting-on summaries still come from the Waiting On Engine, not from project docs or Memory blobs.

---

## PROJECT RULES

Projects should not require manual status updates whenever possible. Instead, infer progress from: Microsoft 365; GitHub; Conversations; Documents; Calendar; Future integrations. When uncertain, ask Derek rather than guessing. The purpose of projects is not reporting. The purpose of projects is helping Derek know where meaningful progress can be made next.
