## Validation Gate Notes (for Morning Brief use)

- **Lookup rule:** find the row whose date range contains today's date. Do not infer from sequence.
- **Week boundary:** weeks run Monday through Sunday. A Sunday belongs to the week that *began* the preceding Monday.
- **Anchor checks:** Lesson 23 = Jun 1–7, 2026. Lesson 32 = Aug 3–9, 2026. Lesson 40 = Sep 28 – Oct 4, 2026.
- **Day 1 of a lesson week = Monday.** Supplemental resources (talks, videos, art) are delivered Monday only and suppressed Tue–Sun.
- **Cross-year edge:** Lesson 01 begins Dec 29, 2025. Lesson 52 ends Dec 27, 2026; Dec 28–31, 2026 falls outside this manual.
